# AgilePoint NX Mobile App Accelerator

**Source file**: `Clippings/AgilePoint NX Mobile App Accelerator.md`

## Summary

Documents the Mobile App Accelerator service — a tool for rapidly creating preconfigured, custom-branded enterprise mobile apps for iOS and Android without extensive development effort.

## Key Points

- Creates custom-branded enterprise mobile applications with minimal development effort
- Branding support: logos, themes, custom images; consistent experience across iOS and Android
- Preconfigured setup reduces deployment timelines
- Distribution options: public app stores, private enterprise stores, direct internal deployment
- Users of custom apps can manage tasks, access apps, monitor processes, and participate in workflows
