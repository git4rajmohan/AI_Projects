# AgilePoint NX iOS Support

**Source file**: `Clippings/agilepoint-ios-support.md`

## Summary

Documents iOS device support within the AgilePoint NX Mobile App. Covers supported Apple devices, offline functionality on iOS, and custom branding support for iOS apps.

## Key Points

- Supported devices: iPhone and iPad
- Offline support: eForms submitted offline, stored locally, auto-synced when connectivity restores
- Custom branding: supports logos, themes, branding elements on iOS apps via [[mobile-app-accelerator]]
- Full parity with mobile app experience on other platforms
