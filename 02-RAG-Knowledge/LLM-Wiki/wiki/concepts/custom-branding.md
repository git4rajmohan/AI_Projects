# Custom Branding

**Type**: Platform Capability / Deployment Pattern

## Definition

Custom Branding is the capability in [[agilepoint-nx]] to produce mobile applications that reflect an organization's brand identity rather than displaying the AgilePoint product identity. This is delivered through the [[mobile-app-accelerator]].

## Branding Elements

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Logo | App icon and in-app logo |
| Color Scheme | Primary/secondary brand colors throughout the UI |
| Theme | Overall visual style and component styling |
| Images | Splash screens, banners, and media assets |

## Use Case Scenarios

- **Corporate Internal Apps** — Employee-facing apps branded with company identity
- **Customer-Facing Apps** — Client apps that match company's consumer branding
- **Field Service Apps** — Operational apps with department or team branding
- **Department-Specific Apps** — Separate branding per business unit

## Relationship to Customized Apps

Custom branding is one dimension of full application customization. A fully customized mobile app includes:

1. **Branding** — logos, themes, colors (this concept)
2. **Functional Customization** — features, workflows, behavior
3. **UX Design** — tailored user experience and interface
