# AgilePoint NX Enterprise Distribution

**Source file**: `Clippings/AgilePoint NX Enterprise Distribution.md`

## Summary

Documents enterprise-internal distribution of custom mobile apps built with the Mobile App Accelerator. Covers Enterprise Mobile Management (EMM) and private app store distribution methods.

## Key Points

- Two distribution methods: Enterprise Mobile Management (EMM) systems and private app stores
- EMM deployment enforces security policies, manages installation/updates/access on devices
- Private app stores host approved internal apps with centralized control over availability
- Restricted to authorized users only; eliminates dependency on public app stores
- Supports controlled rollout, version management, and security compliance
