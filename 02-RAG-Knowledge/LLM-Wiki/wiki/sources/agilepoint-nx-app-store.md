# AgilePoint NX App Store

**Source file**: `Clippings/AgilePoint NX App Store.md`

## Summary

Documents the App Store module within the NX Portal — a marketplace for discovering, downloading, sharing, and distributing applications built on the platform. Covers prebuilt apps, starter templates, and distribution mechanics.

## Key Points

- Serves as a centralized application marketplace within the platform
- Provides prebuilt apps for common business scenarios and starter templates for process/form-based apps
- Users can search, browse, download, install, and share applications
- Supports buying/selling applications within the AgilePoint ecosystem
- Accelerates development by enabling reuse of existing solutions
