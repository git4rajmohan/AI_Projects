# App Store

**Type**: Module — App Marketplace  
**Parent**: [[nx-portal]]

## What It Is

The App Store is the application marketplace within [[nx-portal]], enabling users to discover, download, install, and share business applications. It provides a curated catalog of ready-made applications and starter templates.

## Key Capabilities

- Browse and search prebuilt applications and starter templates
- Download and install apps directly into the platform
- Share or publish internally built applications
- Reduces development time by providing a starting point

## Content Types

- Prebuilt enterprise applications (ready-to-deploy)
- Starter templates (customizable foundations)
- Community or partner shared apps

## Relationships

- Lives within [[nx-portal]]
- Apps run within [[work-center]] after deployment
- Custom mobile apps have separate distribution via [[mobile-app-accelerator]]
